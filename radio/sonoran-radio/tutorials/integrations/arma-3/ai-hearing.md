# AI Hearing

AI hearing lets nearby AI detect a player's position while they transmit. It is disabled by default.

## Enable AI Hearing

1. As the server or mission administrator, open **Options** > **Addon Options**.
2. Select **Sonoran Radio** > **AI Hearing**.
3. Enable **AI can hear transmissions**.
4. Set **Transmission hearing range** between 5 and 100 meters. The default is 20 meters.

Keep the desktop overlay open and use Sonoran Radio's push-to-talk control. Nearby AI can detect the transmission; AI closer to you receive a clearer indication of your position.

<details>
<summary>AI does not react</summary>

1. Confirm AI hearing is enabled for the mission.
2. Confirm the player has the Sonoran Radio mod loaded and the desktop overlay open.
3. Transmit through Sonoran Radio while a living AI unit is within the configured range.

Arma proximity voice does not trigger this feature. AI already aware of your location may not visibly react again.

</details>
