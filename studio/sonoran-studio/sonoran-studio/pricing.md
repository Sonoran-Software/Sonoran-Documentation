# Pricing

## What is included in the free version?

Sonoran Studio's free version includes everything you need to get started with customizable widgets for your livestream.

## What does the paid version unlock?

Our paid version includes additional items:

* Additional customization themes
* Branding removal
* Smart lighting integration
* Streamer.bot integration

## How much is the paid version?

Sonoran Studio's paid version has two payment options:

### Personal License

Individual streamers can purchase a license for $7.99/mo or $18/quarter. This license unlocks the full Studio for any community.

### Sonoran One

Communities with [Sonoran One](https://sonoran.one/) unlock the paid version of Sonoran Studio for all community members, while they're logged into the community's CAD or Radio.

Sonoran One is available for $37.50/mo ($112.50 paid upfront for quarterly) or  $50/mo. This includes:

* Sonoran CAD Pro
* Sonoran CMS Pro
* Sonoran Radio Pro
* FiveM Hosting
* Sonoran Studio Pro for all members
* Exclusive Discord DM support
