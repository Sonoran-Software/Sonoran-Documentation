# Release Status and FAQ

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Release Candidate**

Street Signs is not yet publicly released. The free release is limited to the Highway Sign controller while the remaining model packs and media are prepared.
{% endhint %}

## Current Status

The in-game editor, server-authoritative sign changes, permissions, Discord logging, hosted iframe controller, CAD panel integration, developer API, and optional Power Grid bridge are implemented in the release candidate.

Final launch certification still requires live FXServer testing with Sonoran CAD staging, the Power Grid resource, QB/ESX job data, and a real Discord webhook. In-game screenshots and the product video remain capture tasks after that validation.

## Current Free Version

The free version contains the Highway Sign controller only. US/UK grid signs, billboards, street signs, and vehicle-mounted signs are future expansion packs and are not advertised as live features.
