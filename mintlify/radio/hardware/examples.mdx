Zello's own certification establishes support in Zello, not in Sonoran Radio.
Neither a product family name nor matching connector establishes button support.
An accessory can play received audio while its button or microphone remains unsupported.

## What a verified result must include

Record accessory model/firmware, phone and OS, Sonoran app build, protocol,
microphone route, speaker route, and test date. Test press/release, rapid taps,
initially held buttons, settings suppression, lost connection while held, reconnect,
incoming calls, idle sleep, and the two-user allocation limit.

Record foreground, background reception, and locked-screen transmission separately.
A foreground pass must not be promoted to a locked-screen claim. Public tester
reports should become exact tested combinations, with known limitations retained.
