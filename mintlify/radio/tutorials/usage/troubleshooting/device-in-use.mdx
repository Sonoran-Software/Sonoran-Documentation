## Device In Use Error {#device-in-use-error}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3e/3e3adffb44240f27b35451da75f02dcabcde67865dd94b7fe7ec0b05360083df.png" alt="" width="222" />

*Sonoran Radio: Device In Use*



When connecting to the radio, your microphone may be locked in "exclusive mode" by another program.

### 1. Access the Sound Tab {#id-1.-access-the-sound-tab}

* Open the `Control Panel` and select `Hardware and Sound`
* Select `Manage Audio Devices`



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/44/44bea3324a6c34bb26814aec417b574ca81182e7286c8030ba95923393cbf07a.png" alt="" width="375" />

 

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/ae/aeb95c59698988a0582958406e88bc02647cf5e1f9baba2eba0ced7e6640eeae.png" alt="" width="375" />



### 2. Disable Exclusive Mode {#id-2.-disable-exclusive-mode}

Select the microphone > `Properties` > `Advanced` > Disable (un-check) `Allow applications to take exclusive control of this device`.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/e5/e55a9220d0cb6897a7ac91da644ea3f3e5331c9a11f9561bde3c163879181dbf.png" alt="" width="375" />

*Sonoran Radio: Disable Exclusive Mic Mode*
