## Creating Channel Groups {#creating-channel-groups}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/40/400095706f6cf9a93404204fb7b62b3e893d7875bfb19527edf35bbf527428cc.png" alt="Channels &amp; Groups overview, Basic selected" />



Channel groups allow communities to organize channels in separated categories.

Channel groups are configured in the **Admin > Channels & Groups** tab.

### Add a Group {#add-a-group}

Select **Add Group** above the group list.

Enter a group name and select **Save**.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/8a/8af8d773fdef6b9806f024db52a35ad2a2070f2c0e154bdc25fd0cef286b683a.png" alt="Add group dialog" />

*Sonoran Radio - Add Channel Group*



### Edit or Delete a Group {#edit-or-delete-a-group}

Select the pencil icon beside a group to rename it.

Select the trash icon on the group to remove it.

Re-order the channel groups via drag-and-drop.

### Shared Channels {#shared-channels}

Shared channels let you create a single channel that exists across multiple groups.

#### Setup {#setup}

In this example, there are three radio groups — `City`, `Highway`, and `County`. Each group has its own `Interops` channel, but these channels are linked together so that any transmission on one `Interops` channel is heard across all three groups.

This setup allows seamless communication between departments while maintaining their individual group structures.

1. **Determine the "Primary" Channel**

* One channel must be chosen as the "Primary" channel that the other channels link to. In this example, we will select the `City` group's `Interops`.

2. **Link the First Channel to Primary**

* In the `Highway` group, edit the `Highway Interops` channel. Toggle the channel mode to `Shared` and select the `City Interops` channel (our "primary" channel).

3. **Link the Second Channel to Primary**

* In the `County` group, edit the `County Interops` channel. Toggle the channel mode to `Shared` and select the `City Interops` channel (our "primary" channel).

Now, any user in the City, Highway, or County Interops channel will appear in all three and any transmissions in one will be heard in all three.

## Channels {#channels}

Channels are inside of groups and are what users connect to in order to communicate.

You can create a channel in the **Admin > Channels & Groups** tab, or right inside of the `Dispatch` panel.

* Select the channel name to open its editor.
* Select **Add Channel** in a group to create a channel. You can also create a channel from **Dispatch**.
* Open the channel and select the trash icon to delete it.

## Channel Options {#channel-options}



<Accordion title="Preview the AI Text-To-Speech">



In **Admin > Channels & Groups**, select the play icon beside a channel to hear its name.

If the preview is unavailable, save the channel again to regenerate its audio.

Users can enable or disable the channel text-to-speech playing when switching channels in their settings menu.

![Channels and Groups with the Play channel name control beside the Dispatch channel](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/43/43681df4b5f37892c30157376a2197e5cb70267e93cd9692ae4664e2fc9ba72d.png) ![](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/84/84395bfd55538ae31842e1a7d4be7a5180fc7ed8fc107a1c5b3a6094e3a90287.png)



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="Customize the AI Pronunciation">



By toggling on the `Advanced` mode, you can manually adjust how the AI pronounces the channel name, which can be helpful for abbreviations and uncommon words.

![Edit channel showing editable AI pronunciation](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/1d/1dd6085706c6e476c56bc35081f820f578b3b7d2a28cd9efc800ae3d11319124.png)



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="Setting the Voice Effect">



Use the `Voice Effect` dropdown to set the [custom voice effect style](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-voice-effects) for the channel.

![Edit channel showing selected voice profile](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/63/63ccca83ea3f865ac04ca277917d6e1eb8c06d4fdafb552af3880e0719e80178.png)



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="Toggle Talkover Protection">



Toggle the `Talkover` prevention button to allow or prevent multiple users from talking at once.

With talkover prevented, the [talkover error sound](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-sfx) will play and prevent the user from activating their mic.

![Edit channel showing enabled/disabled talkover control](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/af/afa23d5ef28b4f424fde8e0834ec8eb83c08d3b688be310f222c80c438ae4634.png)



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="Restrict Channel Visibility">



Switch the channel's visibility to `Public` (everyone can see and connect) or `Private` to restrict which users can connect and transmit.

Private channels will appear in the [community member permissions tab](/radio/tutorials/getting-started/invite-and-manage-users), or be [automatically managed with Sonoran CMS](/radio/tutorials/integrations/sonoran-cms).

![Edit channel showing public/private visibility](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/74/74d76a256011fbaf613eee19d9824fa1a2597d794311fd80bde1abfe09f6c688.png)



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="Customize Channel Frequency">



With `Advanced` mode toggled, you can also customize the receive and transmit frequencies for more realism.
