## Vanity URLs {#vanity-urls}

Open **Admin > Custom Domain** and select **Free Vanity URL** to copy it.

If your community is Plus or Pro, your vanity URL will match your community's custom ID

## Custom Domain {#custom-domain}

<Warning>

Custom Domain requires a **pro** subscription.\
[Learn more about our paid plans!](/radio/pricing/pricing-faq/standalone-pricing)
</Warning>

### 1. Enter your Custom Domain {#id-1.-enter-your-custom-domain}

In **Admin > Custom Domain**, enter a root domain such as `example.com` or a subdomain such as `radio.example.com`. Select the arrow beside the field to save the address and show its DNS records.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/0a/0a571788f9739f22c5e4cd18d9660861c3305715123432d43cf6ebeb40dfda75.png" alt="Custom Domain modal showing the domain-name field" />



### 2. Configure DNS Records for you Domain {#id-2.-configure-dns-records-for-you-domain}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/72/72f1e358aeb21da95ff09d9d1c8e7dc78e99716a4b5d5cdac5ff8e584c0d8643.png" alt="Custom Domain modal showing DNS verification instructions" />



Add the displayed DNS records with your domain provider. In Cloudflare, set the records to **DNS only**.

Having trouble? Contact your domain name provider.

### 3. Verify the Custom Domain {#id-3.-save-the-custom-domain}

Select **Verify Custom Domain** after adding the DNS records. When Radio confirms the records, select **Try it out** to open your custom domain.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/7e/7e9167e695fb449b902c8983d01054e3cb838624f10f553c6e21709bb3533679.png" alt="Custom Domain verification success with the Try it out link" />
