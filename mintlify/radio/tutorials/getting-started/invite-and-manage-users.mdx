## Automatic Join, Kick, Ban, and Permission Sync {#automatic-join-kick-ban-and-permission-sync}

Sonoran CMS allows you to automatically add and manage user permissions with user applications, sync them with Discord roles, and more!



<Card title="Sonoran CMS" href="/radio/tutorials/integrations/sonoran-cms" />



If you are building your own integration, Sonoran Radio also exposes API endpoints to approve members, kick members, ban members, update member display names, and set member permissions.



<Card title="Radio User Endpoints" href="/radio/developer-documentation/developer-documentation/api-endpoints-v2/users" />



***

## Discord Role Sync {#discord-role-sync}

Choose Discord role permissions in **Admin > Discord**, or select **Discord permissions** in **Admin > Members**.



<Card title="Discord" href="/radio/tutorials/integrations/discord-bot-integration" />



***

## FiveM Management {#fivem-management}

All user permission management, including inviting and approving members, can be automated with FiveM ACE permissions.

## Manual User Management {#manual-user-management}

### Inviting Members {#inviting-members}

Copy the **Community ID** or **Vanity URL** from **Admin > Info**.\
Communities can also [customize this community ID](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-domain-vanity-url#vanity-urls).



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/8b/8b2951dd9c39da95438df010d348ed5e232fcc39f59c1bb109e4e7fba3fd9b7d.png" alt="Community Info with Community ID and Vanity URL, used for inviting users" />

*Sonoran Radio: Community ID*



When logged into Sonoran Radio, they can select the `+` button in the portal to paste in the `Community ID`.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/63/63fd4b6db1c4a79956d72931236b1428c5999d0eb279148ec74dc984084a8e13.png" alt="" width="170" />

*Sonoran Radio: Join Community*



### Approving Members {#approving-members}

When a member joins your community with an invite code, they will be pending until approved.

With the **Approve Members** permission, open **Admin > Members**, select **Pending**, and open the member’s account. Select **Approve**.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/81/81c58edbc2e6c2067ed529cf2978291463a445bb9de0767c5f70c0349a858a9f.png" alt="Admin Members with Pending selected and a sample account listed" />

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/0a/0a584ffd340132e4b171d84388a5feb452f630d23529e166a51616727ce4a2bf.png" alt="Pending member account with Approve and Ban actions" />

*Sonoran Radio - Approve Members*



### Manage User Permissions {#manage-user-permissions}

In **Admin > Members**, open the member’s account, choose **Permissions**, and select **Save** after making changes.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/32/3267538ed0d77e2ac92c128ce00f022b203a57f4e0ff208d8dd03f2175cae5d8.png" alt="Edit account on the Permissions tab" />
