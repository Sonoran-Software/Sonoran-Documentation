Use the [Sonoran Radio desktop app and overlay](/radio/tutorials/usage/desktop-overlay) while playing Roblox.

For **Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC)**, connect your private server to add live player positions and location-based radio signal:

1. [Link your server and Roblox account](/radio/game-integrations/roblox/erlc-setup).
2. [Place virtual towers and check player coverage](/radio/game-integrations/roblox/erlc-signal-towers).
3. [Open the desktop overlay](/radio/tutorials/usage/desktop-overlay#open-the-overlay).

ER:LC does not require an in-game resource. The ER:LC integration applies to ER:LC private servers.

Use [Dispatch AI for ER:LC](/radio/game-integrations/roblox/erlc) for voice channel controls, timers, CAD actions, and emergency call readouts.
