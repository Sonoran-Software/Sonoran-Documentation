## What are Degrade Zones? {#what-are-degrade-zones}

Degradation zones are areas like tunnels, underground areas, or mountain valleys that would experience signal loss in a realistic scenario. [Custom voice effects](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-voice-effects) will distort the audio more as signal loss increases.

## Creating and Modifying Zones {#creating-and-modifying-zones}

### Via Radio Panel {#via-radio-panel}



<Accordion title="Degrade Zones via Panel">



1. Open **Customization** > **Game Integration** > **FiveM**.
2. Under **FiveM Zones**, choose your **Server** and select **Degrade Zones**.
3. Use the polygon, rectangle, or circle tool to draw a zone.
4. Select the zone to set its name, Z coordinates (floor and ceiling height), and degrade strength.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Zone Configuration Options">



In the `Degradation Zones` menu, you can specify the strength and add points to create a 3D zone.

When creating a zone you can visualize via the drawn polygon and manipulate and move your last placed point using the on-screen controls



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/e4/e4338e1596ad482faf16002a488df1891b135401f54c0243f77367e6cbaaeed7.png" alt="" width="222" />

*Sonoran Radio - Degradation Zone Options*

 

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/ea/ea635ab5a854e1742677ddac208c042f6cc87c03cba87e561552c8313c02bfde.png" alt="" width="375" />

*Sonoran Radio - Degradation Zone Poly*



`Strength`\
\- The percent the signal strength will be cut by when inside the zone (0-1).\
\- Example: 0.5 = 50% [voice effect](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-voice-effects) degradation

`Add Point to Zone`\
\- Adds another point to the 3D zone

`Undo Last Point`\
\- Removes the last place point in the 3D zone

`Min Z`\
\- The minimum Z value (floor height) of the 3D zone

`Max Z`\
\- The maximum Z value (ceiling height) of the 3D zone

`Finish Zone Creation`\
\- Finishes the 3D zone and saves to the `tunnels.json` file

`Cancel Zone Creation`\
\- Cancels the 3D zone editor and closes the menu



</Accordion>





<Accordion title="3. View and Remove Configured Zones">



Select `Edit Degradation Zone` in the menu and toggle on `Show Zones`. All configured zones on the map will be displayed in green. If the config.lua's `debug` mode is set to `true`, all zones will also be made visible.

Use the `Select Zone` menu option to swap back and forth between zones. Your selected zone will be displayed in red.

Use the `Delete Zone` menu option to delete the selected (red) zone.
