## Does the vocoder require Pro? {#does-the-vocoder-require-pro}

**Server AMBE vocoding requires Sonoran Radio Pro.** It processes community voice effects on the server and adds a small network delay. Codec2 vocoding and other custom voice effects remain available on Free, Plus, and Pro.

See [Custom Voice Effects](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-panel/custom-voice-effects#vocoder) for setup instructions, or [View and Compare Plans](/radio/pricing/pricing-faq/standalone-pricing#server-ambe-vocoder) for the plan comparison.
