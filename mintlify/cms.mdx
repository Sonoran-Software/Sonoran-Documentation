## What is Sonoran CMS? {#what-is-sonoran-cms}

Sonoran CMS is the all-in-one management system built for gaming communities.

With advanced applications, Discord role sync, FiveM and ER:LC game panels, a built-in file drive, and more—Sonoran CMS streamlines your operations like never before.



<Card title="[getting-started](tutorials/getting-started/)" href="/cms/tutorials/getting-started" />



### 📂 [CMS Document Drive](/cms/tutorials/your-drive-and-documents)

Manage your community's documents, spreadsheets, and presentations natively in the CMS!

[Create and live edit documents](/cms/tutorials/your-drive-and-documents) to share with members based on rank. Sonoran CMS allows you to watermark documents as sensitive, organize files with folders, and more!

## ✍️ [Custom Applications and Forms](/cms/tutorials/forms/creating-custom-forms)

[Create fully customizable member applications](/cms/tutorials/forms/creating-custom-forms), forms for tracking, and more!

Whether it's a new member application, promotion request, or ban appeal, Sonoran CMS allows you to customize it all.

[AI processing can automatically move forms](/cms/tutorials/forms/ai-form-processing) to various stages based on your requirements.

Each form stage has [powerful automations](/cms/tutorials/forms/creating-custom-forms#form-stages) to add/remove permissions, send push notifications, Discord webhooks or DMs, and more!
