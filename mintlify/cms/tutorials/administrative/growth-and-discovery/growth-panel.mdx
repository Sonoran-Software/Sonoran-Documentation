## Community Leaderboard {#community-leaderboard}

The leaderboard at the top of the growth panel displays your global rankings compared to other CMS communities.

These placements are used to [rank your community in the discovery page](/cms/tutorials/administrative/growth-and-discovery/discovery), helping to drive new users to your community!

## Community Metrics {#community-metrics}

The metrics panel offers historical metrics and performance graphs for your community.\
This allows administrators to easily track their community's growth, decline, or stagnation.

* New Members
  * Number of new members joining the community
* Lost Members
  * Number of members that left the community
* Active Members
  * Unique user visitors per day
* Form Submissions
  * Number of new form submissions on each custom form
* Website Views
  * Number of unique visitors per day on each custom webpage
* Discovery
  * Number of unique views and clicks from the [community discovery system](/cms/tutorials/administrative/growth-and-discovery/discovery)
* URL Shortener
  * Number of visits to each link from the[ URL shortener](/cms/tutorials/administrative/url-shortener)
