<Warning>

Only the community owner can delete or transfer the community.
</Warning>

## How-To: Deleting a Community {#how-to-deleting-a-community}

### 1. Access the Administrative Panel {#id-1.-access-the-administrative-panel}

Navigate to the `Administrative Panel`

![Sonoran CMS - Administrative Panel First View](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/98/980c75773ca3b487cea59bcf3cd47a59b3748583a85f318d16b7ad0d7e066ddc.png)

### 2. Navigate to the Limits View {#id-2.-navigate-to-the-limits-view}

Under the `Community` tab, select `Customization`.

![Sonoran CMS - Limits View](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/9e/9e4389825d0dd9a098d95ee3476a51fa49083465eeecebe2ca62e72852329944.png)

Scroll to the bottom to the `Advanced` section.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/39/39ef2ced8042d05164d847bab1cd68b94ba547c55ba796b178f232f4599f3c8c.png" alt="" />



### 3. Delete Community {#id-3.-delete-community}

Select the **Delete Community** button located in the `Limits` tab and follow the steps it provides.

![Sonoran CMS - First Community Deletion Confirmation](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3d/3df5ae16ecfb7bbb76b8f8dbfd569294016dc581e108162f71b006c830fece74.png)

## How-To: Transferring a Community {#how-to-transferring-a-community}

### 1. Access the Administrative Panel {#id-1.-access-the-administrative-panel-1}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/98/980c75773ca3b487cea59bcf3cd47a59b3748583a85f318d16b7ad0d7e066ddc.png" alt="" />

*Sonoran CMS - Administrative Panel*



### 2. Navigate to the Customizations Panel {#id-2.-navigate-to-the-customizations-panel}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/9e/9e4389825d0dd9a098d95ee3476a51fa49083465eeecebe2ca62e72852329944.png" alt="" />

*Sonoran CMS - Community Limits View*



### 3. Transfer Community {#id-3.-transfer-community}

Select the "Transfer Community" button located in the `Advanced` section and follow the steps provided.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/d3/d30f77bd7ef1af6a87786dc006c2a167e31af85a87c39ba358802b6e50800fec.png" alt="" />

*Sonoran CMS - Transfer Community Selection*



### 4. Finalize Transfer {#id-4.-finalize-transfer}

Once you submit for the transfer in Sonoran CMS you'll receive an email regarding the transfer, this is to confirm and finalize it. Once you get the email click the button located on the bottom which will take you to Sonoran CMS to finalize it. You'll be notified upon full load if the transfer went through. The new owner will receive an email stating their new ownership.



<img src="https://i.imgur.com/WoAr6lW.png" alt="" />

*Sonoran CMS - Transfer Community Initiation Email*





<img src="https://i.imgur.com/yALB2Ru.png" alt="" />

*Sonoran CMS - Transfer Community Completion Email*



<Info>

You have **30** minutes from when you initiate the transfer to use the button in the transfer confirmation email.
</Info>
