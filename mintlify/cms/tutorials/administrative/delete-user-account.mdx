### 1. Access your Sonoran Software Account {#id-1.-access-your-sonoran-software-account}

You can access your Sonoran Software account by going to [account.sonoransoftware.com](https://account.sonoransoftware.com), or by selecting "Profile" in the side menu then "Edit Account" on the next page.

![Sonoran CMS - Modify Account](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/0d/0d34ebba3ee6e4bc947cf71cf729add9e287db02540cdf7f3eeaf05f2a0a9efb.png)

### 2. Send an Account Removal Email {#id-2.-send-an-account-removal-email}

On the Sonoran Account website, select `Account` > `Advanced` > `Delete Account`

This will send an email confirmation to the address on file.

![Sonoran Account - Delete Account](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3d/3d66eed3e6dc968868876a8ca2c44e8bd6a3c771c146ca900e95198feef15841.png)

### 3. Verify your Email {#id-3.-verify-your-email}

Check your email for an account removal request and click the "Remove Account" button.\
Having trouble seeing it? Be sure to check your spam/junk folder.

![Sonoran Account - Removal Email](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/bb/bb5da04f0e63f6ffe4b3deefc049d5e900f6c73bcc1a18395a07613f8b6c435e.png)
