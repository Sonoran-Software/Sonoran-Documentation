<Info>

If you already have an account on [sonorancad.com](https://sonorancad.com/) and/or [account.sonoransoftware.com](https://account.sonoransoftware.com) you can simply [login](https://sonorancms.com/#/login) instead of creating an account.
</Info>

### 1. Navigate to the Login Page {#id-1.-navigate-to-the-login-page}

On the website or app, select your Login method.

When viewing a specific community, they may have [restricted login options](/cms/tutorials/customization/community-branding-and-settings#allowed-login-methods).

![The account registration panel allows users to input their information.](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/21/2111d2b3d81876f0ba5378767e381faa74f0931fc98b350aefef0e02ae6f1e6d.png)

### 2. Enter Your Account Information {#id-2.-enter-your-account-information}

Enter in your email, username, and password. Then, select the **Register** button.

![Verification emails are sent for user registration.](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/af/afd2b8ad619c2026e3030ab50888d71665f534a2d3908427d2b1854ea07f25d3.png)

### 3. Verify Your Email Address {#id-3.-verify-your-email-address}

<Warning>

Account verification emails may not be shown in your primary inbox.

If you're having trouble finding the email, check your spam, junk, promotions, or all mail folder.
</Warning>

Check your inbox for an email from Sonoran CMS.\
\
Select the **Verify Email** button, and your account will be automatically verified.

![Account verification emails are sent as shown above.](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/ee/eed52c213d5390050c85986640a7ccf7557b23219d536d2e4e02fdbebe91fc92.png)
