## Select and Manage a Player {#select-and-manage-a-player}

Click on any online player to interact.

* Copy unique identifiers
* Warn the player in-game
* Kick the player from the server
* View their [characters](/cms/integration-capabilities/fivem-game-panel/qbcore-and-qbox-panel/characters)

## Warn a Player {#warn-a-player}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3a/3a30a0eae386cb85d62e290c5aa1b6a4d2e24333290f70f69ba2ad52730d596f.png" alt="" width="563" />



Selecting `Warn` on a player will prompt you to add a reason message. The player will then receive a warning notice in-game, requiring them to hold down `SPACE` for ten seconds to dismiss.
