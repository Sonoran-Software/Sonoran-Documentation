## What is Discord Rich Presence? {#what-is-discord-rich-presence}

When running our [desktop application](/cms/download), Discord can automatically detect and display information about your community.

## Customizing your Rich Presence {#customizing-your-rich-presence}

![Sonoran CMS - Discord Rich Presence Customization](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/b2/b2e54d75a156d3cd3da560b53e0ca0154ba5f3983e83fc31576dd7b892b8443f.png)

### Customizing Buttons {#customizing-buttons}

Discord presence currently allows for two customizable buttons.

Navigate to Administrator Panel > Customization > Community Customization > Discord Rich Presence Customization

#### For communities on the **Standard** plan or higher: {#for-communities-on-the-standard-plan-or-higher}

\- Button #2 may be customized to any [Sonoran CAD](/cad) or [Sonoran CMS](/cms) invitation link.\
\- Ex: `https://sonorancad.com/#/?comid=mycommunityid` or `https://sonorancms.com/#/?comid=mycommunityid`

#### For communities on the Pro plan: {#for-communities-on-the-pro-plan}

\- Both buttons may be customized to any URL desired.\
\- The Icon and title can also be customized with a Discord developer application.

### Customizing the Icon {#customizing-the-icon}

Communities on the **Pro** version may customize the rich presence icon:

#### 1. Create a new Discord Application {#id-1.-create-a-new-discord-application}

On [https://discord.com/developers/applications](https://discord.com/developers/applications) create a new application

![Discord Developer - New Application](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/26/26b667205df33634d7a2ac9fb93ba25e7f1188f2b4ba60a8896b18518909f8ad.png)

#### 2. Copy your Client ID {#id-2.-copy-your-client-id}

Under OAuth2, copy your `Client ID`

![Discord Developer - Application Client ID](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3f/3ffd20ee73ea6b40581ca351182b88d5a6c08523e3688fffcd2bf3d3a1c6682f.png)

#### 3. Add an Icon {#id-3.-add-an-icon}

Next, upload an icon for your new application. Be sure to copy down the name of the icon for later.

![Discord Developer - Application Icon](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/26/26c82bfafb80fa2ce860d1dad6298322fd08143f0288052cfa6a5c9cfe55f293.png)

#### 4. Configure in Sonoran CMS {#id-4.-configure-in-sonoran-cms}

Back in the admin customization menu, we can paste the Discord application's Client ID and Icon name.

![Sonoran CMS - Custom Discord Presence Config](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/7f/7f336dd09b4e1826e4bd5424b2620cc299f0eb2d9db388410c4d96d70cad9fba.png)

Once saved, your Discord presence for all community members will reflect your custom icon, title, and buttons.

![Sonoran CAD - Custom Discord Presence View](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/94/94647dcd952886e80cc961ba4af076686ca77ebf94abad4f88c615d7cfaaa174.png)

## Toggle On/Off Rich Presence  {#toggle-on-off-rich-presence}

### Community Toggle  {#community-toggle}

To hide the invite button for your community's Discord Rich Presence, simply leave the button text and URL blank.

### User Toggle  {#user-toggle}

In the profile dropdown, users can toggle this display on or off for their individual desktop client.

![Sonoran CMS Profile Dropdown - Disable Discord's Rich Presence](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/34/34948b28a3d6f0b664cad11f1f88961473a95b73a82b43973d1ccccbb9d6a2a0.png)
