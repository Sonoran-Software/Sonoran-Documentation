See the guide below for claiming the `Customer`role in our Discord.

#### Customer Lobby <span id="customer-lobby" /> {#customer-lobby}

The `Customer` role grants users access to the #customer-lobby channel.

#### 1. Retrieve your Subscription ID <span id="id-1-retrieve-your-subscription-id" /> {#id-1-retrieve-your-subscription-id}

In the Sonoran CMS billing center, copy the subscription ID from the subscriptions table:

![Sonoran CMS's Billing Center](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/a3/a334456e38eea01f8a3e636aabb68c0f05c8b1f76a5db6bb172e5561be05b3f7.png)

#### 2. Retrieve your Discord Tag <span id="id-2-retrieve-your-discord-tag" /> {#id-2-retrieve-your-discord-tag}

In Discord, click your username to copy your Discord tag.

![Copy Discord Tag](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/ce/ce70fdf5565356b74db4cc3c99ac1fa2eaf14eae7289f31fac6c15edae5839a4.png)

#### 3. Enter your Information <span id="id-3-enter-your-information" /> {#id-3-enter-your-information}

On our [support website](https://support.sonoransoftware.com/), select the "Discord Role" button, select CMS Customer and paste in your information.

![Sonoran Support - Request Discord Role](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/62/6239853c659638785eda49550e823f62ff939e00dee4d32c4c46ba747b34b56a.png)
