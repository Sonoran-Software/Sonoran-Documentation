## What is Sonoran CAD? {#what-is-sonoran-cad}



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/03/036bd368fc2dce6dec32bb8df1f14eaad758f2118367030bb22710ac8084748b.png" alt="" width="375" />



Sonoran CAD is the largest, most popular dispatch and records management system for gaming communities.

With seamless integrations for FiveM, ER:LC on Roblox, and more, it delivers unmatched power, speed, and customization—taking your roleplay experience to the next level.



<Card title="getting-started" href="/cad/tutorials/getting-started" />



### ✨ [Dispatch AI](/radio/tutorials/usage/dispatch-ai) {#dispatch-ai}

Dispatch AI is our most advanced integration to date—bringing a real-time AI dispatcher directly into your community’s CAD.

When paired with [Sonoran Radio](/radio), units can create calls, run license plates, update their status, and more—all automatically through their radio transmissions.
