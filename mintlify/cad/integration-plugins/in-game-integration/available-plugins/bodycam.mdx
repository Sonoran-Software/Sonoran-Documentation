## What is the live Body Camera? {#what-is-the-live-body-camera}

View live video from in-game users accessible through the [live map](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#live-map), [active units preview](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#active-units), or a [dedicated window](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#window).

## Activation Guide {#activation-guide}

### Download and Install the Resource {#id-1.-download-and-install-the-resource}

<Info>

This submodule is already **enabled by default** when installing the [Sonoran CAD FiveM resource](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/fivem-installation).

</Info>

### Adjust the Configuration {#id-2.-adjust-the-configuration}

Open **Admin > Integrations > FiveM**, select the server, then open **Body Camera**. Enable the plugin with the header toggle and review the settings below. Changes save automatically. When saved, run `exec @sonorancad/sonorancad.cfg` in your server console to apply them.

See [Submodule Configuration](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/submodule-configuration) for requirements, reset, and migration details.

## In-Game Usage {#in-game-usage}

When in-game, units [must also be actively signed into the dispatch, police, fire, or EMS panel](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#unit-duty-requirement).

On first usage, players will be prompted to grant permission for the bodycam:

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/97/975534ab44f7dff632a9ab9d078ea4af38af20b5989e603217926de14eb0fb09.png" alt="" />

#### Commands {#commands}

In-game commands can be used to

* `/bodycam` Toggle the bodycam on or off
* `/bodycam sound` Displays your current local sound volume
* `/bodycam sound 0` Mutes bodycam sounds locally; use a value from `0` to `1`, such as `/bodycam sound 0.2` for 20% volume
* `/bodycam frequency` Displays your current beep interval in seconds
* `/bodycam frequency 30` Sets your bodycam beep interval to 30 seconds; accepts whole seconds from `1` to `3600`
* `/bodycam anim` Toggles the [bodycam animation](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#animation) on and off locally
* `/bodycam overlay` Toggles the [bodycam overlay](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#body-camera-overlay) on and off locally
* `/bodycam forceoff` Enables the [force-off state](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#force-off), if your server has granted you permission

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/77/7788e984748abdb91bfcc6ea540acd207dbf4f9b86d13b2f06bc4896c772c4e6.png" alt="" />

### Keybind {#keybind}

Users can customize a keybind to toggle their bodycams on and off.

Navigate to **Settings** > **Keybinds** > **FiveM** and look for the keybind **Toggle BodyCam** under the resource `sonorancad`.

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/11/11ab0b96863292b86857e061ac942f45bb129ca75de3faad3238acf7a4acdb49.png" alt="" />

#### Body Camera Overlay {#body-camera-overlay}

The on-screen overlay has a generic camera housing, a blinking status light, and a gold/red Sonoran CAD logo by default.

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/23/2392fa98bfa5b8859a872c6fe4dbaa2e74030912f44f3e5f29395a4789e540b9.gif" alt="Sonoran CAD in-game body camera overlay with a gold/red logo and blinking red status light." width="480" />

To swap the logo:

1. In `sonorancad/core/client_nui/img/`, copy `bodycam_logo_standard.png` (red/white), `bodycam_logo_gold.png` (gold/red), or your own transparent PNG and name the copy `bodycam_logo.png`.
2. Restart `sonorancad`.

No configuration change is needed. Automatic updates preserve `bodycam_logo.png`; delete it to restore the default gold/red logo.

### Turning Off a Camera Being Viewed {#turning-off-a-camera-being-viewed}

The normal `/bodycam` toggle cannot turn your camera off while someone is viewing it in CAD. Have all viewers close the camera window and stop viewing its live map or active-unit preview, then try again. If a viewer disconnects unexpectedly, allow a short delay for the connection to clear.

If your server has granted you [force-off access](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/available-plugins/bodycam#force-off), you can use `/bodycam forceoff` to stop it while viewers are connected. The in-game message only suggests that command when you have access; otherwise, wait until all viewers stop watching.

Closing or losing one viewer does not end the camera feed for other viewers or interrupt an ongoing recording.

### Beeps {#beeps}

The FiveM body camera plays periodic beeps while activated. These settings are separate from the desktop/web screen-sharing camera, even when CAD is opened inside an in-game tablet. Changing the CAD **Modify Identifier > Bodycam** sound settings does not change FiveM's bodycam sounds.

#### Per-Player Commands {#per-player-commands}

* `/bodycam sound 0` or `/bodycam sound 0.0` mutes local bodycam sounds, including start/stop and reminder beeps. `/bodycam sound 0.2` restores the default 20% volume.
* `/bodycam sound` displays your current volume. Values from `0` to `1` are accepted.
* `/bodycam frequency 30` sets a 30-second interval. `/bodycam frequency 290` sets a 290-second interval. Use whole seconds from `1` to `3600`; `/bodycam frequency` displays the current value.

A frequency change restarts the reminder countdown using the new interval. It applies to your camera's reminders, including the sound requests sent to nearby players; it does not change other players' camera intervals. Local muting does not prevent nearby players from hearing your camera. Your volume and interval overrides reset when you reconnect or the resource restarts. If your server renamed the `bodycam` command, use its configured name instead.

These commands require the updated FiveM resource. Server administrators must install the update and restart the resource before players can use zero-volume muting or the frequency command.

#### Server Configuration {#server-configuration}

In **Beeps**, set **Beep interval (ms)** to `30000` for 30 seconds or `290000` for 290 seconds. The in-game frequency command uses seconds instead.

**Enable beeps** controls the recurring reminder loop. Turning it off does not silence start/stop tones or nearby cameras. **Beep range** sets how far nearby players can hear your camera.

Changes save automatically. When saved, run `exec @sonorancad/sonorancad.cfg` in your server console to apply them.

### Automatic Activation {#automatic-activation}

Turn on **Turn on with emergency lights** or **Turn on when drawing a weapon** to enable those triggers. Configure eligible items in **Weapons** and required uniform items in **Clothing**.

### Force Off {#force-off}

Use `/bodycam forceoff` to keep your bodycam off, including automatic activation and viewing requests. Use `/bodycam` to turn it back on.

By default, force off requires the `sonorancad.bodycam.forceoff` FiveM ACE permission. CAD account permissions do not grant this access.

#### Grant Access {#grant-access}

Add this example to `server.cfg` (or a permissions file executed by it), replacing `YOUR_LICENSE` with the player's license identifier:

```cfg
add_ace group.sonoran_cad_bodycam sonorancad.bodycam.forceoff allow
add_principal identifier.license:YOUR_LICENSE group.sonoran_cad_bodycam
```

Repeat the `add_principal` line for each player. Restart the server to apply, or run the lines in the server console for immediate access.

#### Disable the Permission Check {#disable-the-permission-check}

Clear **ACE permission to force off** to allow everyone to use `/bodycam forceoff`. To require a different permission, enter that ACE node and use the same node in your `add_ace` rule. Changes save automatically. When saved, run `exec @sonorancad/sonorancad.cfg` in your server console to apply them.

### Unit Duty Requirement {#unit-duty-requirement}

With **Require unit duty** enabled, the player needs an active CAD unit to activate their body camera.

### Animation {#animation}

**Enable animation** controls the animation played when the camera is turned on or off. Players can also toggle their local animation with `/bodycam anim`.

### In-Game Recording {#in-game-recording}

By default, each recording includes a 30-second shadow buffer followed by 1 minute 30 seconds of video, for a maximum total length of 2 minutes. The shadow buffer length can be adjusted with **Shadow buffer seconds**, but the total recording length cannot exceed 2 minutes.

Recording can be started manually using a configurable keybind located under **Keybinds -> FiveM -> Sonoran CAD**. You can also enable automatic bodycam recording through **Auto record events**.

Developers can also trigger bodycam recordings with a [client event](/cad/integration-plugins/in-game-integration/framework-development-documentation/client-events#bodycam-record-toggle).

Recordings can be viewed in the CAD under **Unit Management** > **Body Cam Recordings**

## Attach Clips to Custom Records {#attach-clips-to-custom-records}

In **Admin > Records and Codes > Custom Records**, edit a template, add a **Bodycam clips** field, and save. See [custom record templates](/cad/tutorials/customization/creating-custom-record-and-report-types) for setup.

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/2a/2af7e4b8090583dc2c81d4c76f9951b558f969b194965f6efd41a9599e4d7e63.png" alt="Custom record field settings with Bodycam clips selected as the type." width="320" />

When creating or editing a record, click **Select clip**, choose a recording, then click **Attach clip**. Save the record.

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/f7/f792a234e727f9fe8c8f5531be1eb98d06ec4a697b49b836b468cbdce0c32949.png" alt="Bodycam clip picker showing a FiveM recording and the Attach clip button." />

Play the video directly in the record. The **Paste URL** icon also accepts an HTTPS video link.

<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/1c/1c83d97d1a5a942b7ddb7f53e8274c237427b3dc11fad7c70d9e85928e9adad0.png" alt="FiveM bodycam clip playing inside a custom record." />

CAD-hosted clips expire after **24 hours**; attaching them does not extend storage. Third-party integrations can [register external clips through the API](/cad/api-integration/api-endpoints-v2/general/configuration/register-external-bodycam-recording). Those links remain usable while the host keeps the video available.
