See the call and its location together in Discord. Notifications include the call details and a watermarked aerial image when a matching location image is available.

Before you start, [connect Sonoran Bot to your CAD community](/bot/tutorials/getting-started). For in-game 911 calls, complete [ERLC setup](/cad/integration-plugins/erlc/getting-started) too.

### 1. Check ERLC mode {#id-1.-check-erlc-mode}

Make sure your CAD community is in **ER:LC** mode under **Administration → Advanced → In-Game Integration**.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/fb/fbce76ee84f03f185a5deb552d0c7e99bc2ea4cc05ed6e3080e9c6448d4a915c.png" alt="" width="375" />



### 2. Choose your notifications {#id-2.-choose-your-notifications}

Open **Administration → Advanced → Discord Integration**. Enable the events you want:

* **Emergency Call · Created** — new 911 calls.
* **Dispatch · Created** — new dispatch calls.
* **Dispatch · Modified** — updated dispatch calls.

### 3. Pick a channel {#id-3.-pick-a-channel}

Choose your Discord **Server** and **Channel** for each enabled event. Changes save automatically.

For the 3D aerial images, [dispatch calls must have a postal code set](/cad/integration-plugins/erlc/call-editor-pin-drop). In-game 911 calls already include the required location information.
