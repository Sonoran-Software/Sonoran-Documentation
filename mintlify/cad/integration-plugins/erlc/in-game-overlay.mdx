## Using the In-Game Overlay {#using-the-in-game-overlay}

### Configuring the Hotkey {#configuring-the-hotkey}

The in-game overlay is toggled via custom hotkey. Hotkeys can be configured under **Customization** > **Hotkeys** > **Desktop Overlay**. Pressing this hotkey will show/hide the overlay.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/68/68abfa315540d26a9564a45a4e8039703ba6e686aebeb3ebe4d69c7ef88dfa54.png" alt="" width="375" />



### Overlay Tab {#overlay-tab}

In the desktop app the taskbar also displays a **Overlay** tab that can be clicked to activate the overlay.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/6f/6fcca4552dfd8acdd8364048693eb8d7b0c7292894ade9b27cc4f4a1c756f6ba.png" alt="" />



### Overlay Buttons {#overlay-buttons}

The overlay offers several buttons at the top right.

#### Visibility {#visibility}

Click the **Eye** icon to make the overlay window more or less transparent.

#### Self-Dispatch {#self-dispatch}

Click the **Self Dispatch** button to toggle on access to dispatching features:

* Previous/Next Call
* Attach/Detach from Call

Users must have the **Self Dispatch** permission in order to toggle this on.

#### **Include Emergency Calls** {#include-emergency-calls}

When enabled (requires **Self Dispatch**) emergency calls will also be displayed in the overlay. Pressing the **Attach** button (enter) will automatically convert the emergency call to a dispatch call, attaching your unit.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/e6/e66a8a98c5c3f4cec4d2c268db21b7b7c4f0af5fbbc43b927857d938a21a2ae6.png" alt="" width="359" />



#### Previous/Next Call {#previous-next-call}

When **Self Dispatch** is enabled, use the left and right arrow keys (or icons) to page between active dispatch calls.

#### Attach/Detach from Call {#attach-detach-from-call}

When **Self Dispatch** is enabled, use the **enter** key or press the attach/detach icon to attach or detach from the selected dispatch call.

#### Call Notes {#call-notes}

Click the **Call Notes** button to view the notes tab. Enter in text for live-chat while on a dispatch call.


## ID Check {#id-check}

When you approach a nearby civilian, their username and a **Request ID** button appear at the bottom of the overlay. Click **Request ID** to view their Sonoran CAD character as a Liberty County identification card.

See [ID Check](/cad/integration-plugins/erlc/id-check) for setup requirements and details.
