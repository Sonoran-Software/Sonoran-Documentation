Give members CAD permissions through their Discord roles.

## 1. Connect Discord {#id-1.-connect-discord}

Follow [Connect your server](/cad/integration-plugins/discord-bot-integration), then open **Administration > Advanced > Discord Integration > Role sync**. You can also select **Set up role sync** in **Administration > Accounts**.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/de/de58efd1cab6e2fbbddb32f513dd777e78ac393c4154d14ba70c858f3e748bd6.jpg" alt="CAD Role sync tab showing Police Officer and Dispatcher mappings with edit and remove buttons" />

*Each role shows its Discord server and assigned permissions.*



<Info>

If this community uses Sonoran CMS, configure [CAD permissions in CMS](/cms/integration-capabilities/sonoran-cad-sync). CAD role mapping is disabled for CMS-managed communities.
</Info>

## 2. Add a role {#id-2.-add-a-role}

1. Select **Add role**.
2. Type a role name and select it. Check the role color and server name.
3. Choose permissions under **Access**, **Records**, and **Administration**.
4. Wait for **Saved automatically**, then select **Done**.



<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/1a/1a773fd6184d6ecd11f05bc3ad38e5929a06adefc9f8b6a71db4c51fe273a928.jpg" alt="Searchable Discord role menu showing role names, colors, and their server" />

*Choose the role from the correct Discord server.*





<img src="https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/1f/1f0a2dbaf3aa0db06434f3ef5d5701dc0cfa278062661c1ac1f674e6c7f6ab7c.jpg" alt="Police Officer role mapping with CAD access permissions and Saved automatically confirmation" />

*Choose the permissions this role gives members.*



Under **Records**, expand a template to choose what members can view, create, edit, or delete. See [Account permissions](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/permissions#record-permissions) for permission details.

*Set permissions separately for each record template.*



## 3. Sync members {#id-3.-sync-members}

1. Members join your CAD community.
2. Members run `/sync` in Discord. If their account needs linking, the bot shows them how.

After a mapping changes, all linked members automatically resync **5 minutes after your last edit**. Finishing the sync may take a little longer.

Members receive the combined permissions from all their mapped roles. Sync replaces their CAD permissions, including permissions assigned manually or through a key.

## Edit or remove a mapping {#edit-or-remove-a-mapping}

1. Select the **Edit permissions** icon beside a role to change its permissions. Changes save automatically.
2. To remove a mapping, select the **Remove role** icon and confirm. This removes the mapping, not the Discord role.
