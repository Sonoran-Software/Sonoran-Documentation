### Adding Quick Links {#adding-quick-links}

In the admin customization menu, expand the Quick Links section:

#### 1. Add, edit and remove quick links by specifying the label, link, and color code. {#id-1.-add-edit-and-remove-quick-links-by-specifying-the-label-link-and-color-code}

![Sonoran CAD's quick link customization panel](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/9e/9efecafee324cb3d0b5f55ae8c71a9729eb6ed77e8d242dcf2c4e7fbdb3a1326.png)

#### 2. Be sure to press SAVE {#id-2.-be-sure-to-press-save}

![Save Quick Links](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/e4/e404b0ab40115970b1037ed1a6a52f403fc999f9f90601a751a95c37fd4f6c2a.png)

### Viewing Quick Links {#viewing-quick-links}

Quick links can be found in the taskbar under Documentation > Quick Links

Or via the taskbar search and [pinning](/cad/tutorials/customization/customizing-your-layout#7.-tab-system) system.
