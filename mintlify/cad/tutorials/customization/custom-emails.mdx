---
title: Custom Emails
description: Sonoran CAD allows you to send customized, branded emails when your community
  members register from your custom login page.
---

<Warning>

NOTE: If you have not already, you will need to configure your [custom login page](/cad/tutorials/customization/custom-login-page).
</Warning>

<Info>

Community customizations is not enabled with the free version of Sonoran CAD.\
For more information, see our [pricing](/cad/pricing/faq) or view how to check your community [limits](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/view-your-limits).
</Info>

#### 1. Configure Your Emails {#id-1.-configure-your-emails}

In the admin customization menu, expand the "Custom Community Emails" section.\
Here, you can set the email header, sub-header, and body text.\
\
\&#xNAN;_NOTE:_ Your community logo will automatically be inserted into all emails.

![The email customization panel allows you to enter your own text](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/4b/4ba32b82e213a4696c51f06b545d90a5c8543c6ab871099e8e40cbcc1dc913c0.png)

#### 2. Save and Enjoy! {#id-2.-save-and-enjoy}

Once saved, all user account registration and password recovery emails sent from your custom login page will be customized as configured.

![Custom emails are sent to users as configured](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/c9/c997eedb9a6e3ad9dbdb0ece197c7f170d09a663e96c345c725b5536d276944e.png)
