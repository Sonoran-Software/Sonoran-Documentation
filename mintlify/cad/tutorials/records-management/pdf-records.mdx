### Records {#records}

To download a PDF copy of a criminal or civilian record, select the PDF button in the bottom action bar.

![PDF print options are shown at the bottom of the record viewer](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/99/99c75a949be61a0f12e176dadcf6674ac6355e314b6c67c0924e0f948d25a84c.png)

![PDF records are generated and can be saved](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/08/08ab0e3d68defa97be88577c2c6fa079fb552da0ffd3df05e842119c6be0b84d.png)

### Call Logs {#call-logs}

Prior call logs can also be printed to a PDF record.

1. Navigate to the "Active Calls" window
2. Select the "Call History" tab
3. Click the call and select "Print Call"

![The "Call History" tab's PDF print option is displayed for all users](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/47/479be55f8e9e5cf87ca3b4de78d681bd6b13849ac45a7d832f4ff97eaacd8020.png)
