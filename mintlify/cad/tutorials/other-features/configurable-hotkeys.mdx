<Warning>

Configurable hotkeys are not included with the free version.\
For more information, see our [pricing](/cad/pricing/faq) or view how to check your community [limits](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/view-your-limits).
</Warning>

<Info>

Hotkeys are enabled **globally** on the [Desktop ](/cad/download)application. This means users do not have to have the app or page in focus or visible for the hotkeys to be registered.

For desktop users, we **highly recommend** viewing our [guide on in-game use](/cad/download/steam-browser-workaround)!
</Info>

### Configure your Hotkeys {#configure-your-hotkeys}

In the side menu, open the "Settings" modal to display your hotkey configuration menu.\
Here, you can configure your prefix and hotkey buttons using the drop-downs below.

Once changed, your settings are automatically applied and saved.
