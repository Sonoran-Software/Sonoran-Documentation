## Finding your Community ID {#finding-your-community-id}

Your community ID lets users [join your community manually](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/inviting-users-to-your-cad). You can also copy your free community URL from **Administration > Customization > Custom Domain** and share it so users join automatically when they log in. See [Custom Domain & Vanity URLs](/cad/tutorials/customization/custom-login-page) for details.

The ID can be found in the account menu dropdown once inside of a community.

## Authenticating your Ownership {#authenticating-your-ownership}

While requesting support from an [official Sonoran team member](https://support.sonoransoftware.com/) (via our support portal), you may be asked to authenticate your community's ownership.

<Warning>

Your community's ownership authentication code can only be retrieved from your CAD owner.\
\
This code is used in support tickets to confirm that you are the registered owner of the community. **NEVER** give this code out to anyone that is not a member of the Sonoran support team!
</Warning>

1. In the admin page, navigate to `Advanced` > `Authenticate`
2. Press the "Request Code" button
3. Your ownership authentication code will be displayed on the screen
