---
title: Steam Browser - Clear Cache
description: Learn how to clear your Steam browser's cache.
---

### 1. Open the Settings Page {#id-1.-open-the-settings-page}

In the Steam application, navigate to `Steam` > `Settings`

![Steam - Open Settings](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/af/affad560d329ee2cf2df14fab87202f008fba21edd27b1423c6ff84b6c02812a.png)

### 2. Clear Web Browser Cache {#id-2.-clear-web-browser-cache}

In the settings page, select `Web Browser` > `Delete Web Browser Cache`

![Steam - Clear Browser Cache](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/a8/a84ed19e24c41411cd2ee2482cbf69109839fc0e2a720011514fc6de2c2fb34a.png)
