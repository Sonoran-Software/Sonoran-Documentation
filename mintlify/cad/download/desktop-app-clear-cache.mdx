---
title: Desktop App - Clear Cache
description: This guide covers how to clear your Windows Desktop app cache.
---

You may need to manually clear your Sonoran CAD desktop application's cache for troubleshooting steps. The guide below will walk you through the process.

### 1. Open Your AppData Folder {#id-1.-open-your-appdata-folder}

In the window search bar type `run` and open the run application.\
Enter `appdata` then hit `Ok`<br />

![Windows - Run AppData](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/d7/d7561f584fbacce82c9b8bacc70e4f825c746fc9408d79c6fd96c1e4d606773a.png)

### 2. Delete the Cache Folder {#id-2.-delete-the-cache-folder}

Navigate to `Roaming` > `Sonoran CAD` and delete the `Cache`, `Local Storage`, and `Session Storage` folders.

![Desktop App - Cache Folders](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/76/761dd7dd547968bfcca8802f8aad975dff4e81a7244ec0c0043134a83c51e527.png)
