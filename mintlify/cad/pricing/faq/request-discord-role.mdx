## Available Roles {#available-roles}

### Customer Role {#customer-role}

The `Customer` role grants users access to the `#customer-lobby` channel in our [Discord](https://discord.sonoransoftware.com/).

#### 1. Retrieve your Subscription ID {#id-1.-retrieve-your-subscription-id}

In the billing portaltal, copy the subscription ID from the "Active Subscriptions" table:

![Sonoran CAD's Payment Center](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/aa/aa73714ad098e3906f24a86e408936e5aa55511d25e4e00d4ef7cf0da7e45e2d.png)

#### 2. Retrieve your Discord Tag {#id-2.-retrieve-your-discord-tag}

In Discord, click your username to copy your Discord tag.

![Copy Discord Tag](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/fa/fa4edc2419b0eb02b6d69e7000b5207dc32948d0679126e63f4a96add231c9eb.png)

#### 3. Enter your Information {#id-3.-enter-your-information}

On our [support website](https://support.sonoransoftware.com), select the "Discord Role" button and paste in your information.

![Sonoran Support - Request Discord Role](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/b8/b85a41a71cb33c9bbc1ca7e6f3f28d0c93ae17fb77d0d141f63080e738922869.png)
