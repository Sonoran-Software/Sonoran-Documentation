## Create a New Subscription {#create-a-new-subscription}

### 1. Access the Payment Center {#id-1.-access-the-payment-center}

Access the payment center on the Web or Desktop version of Sonoran CAD by going to [sonorancad.com/billing](https://sonorancad.com/billing), or by viewing the guide below.



<Card title="Accessing the Payment Center" href="/cad/pricing/faq/accessing-the-payment-center" />



### 2. Select the Desired Subscription {#id-2.-select-the-desired-subscription}

In the payment center, select "New Subscription"\
From there, select the monthly subscription you would like to start.

![Sonoran CAD - Payment Center Selection](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/39/39a53377b55bc2571f21d1503d0811e5405f2c47d8c447c079b00811eef4935f.png)

### 3. Complete the Purchase {#id-3.-complete-the-purchase}

After selecting the desired subscription plan, you will be redirected to a Stripe checkout page.\
Here, you can enter your billing information and start the subscription.

![Sonoran CAD - Stripe Checkout](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/70/70624c9b32ad1b63cf6cf723687567116e904c20db55857fc2e747e5b782e0d0.png)

### 4. Set your Community ID {#id-4.-set-your-community-id}

Once you've completed your purchase, set the community ID on the subscription in your billing center.\
[Where can I find my community ID?](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/finding-your-community-id-and-authentication-code)

![Sonoran CAD Payment Center - Set Community ID](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/8f/8f8fd8589fe4c3aa87d72f87d34c4b5b0f04c58df9f9de136c75762eecb7e352.png)

The modal will show options to set the subscription to any community you are currently a part of.

![Payment Center - Set Community ID Prompt](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/3d/3d4594ccca69fbd6ac7584e8d2c14748cffd06faa119cf91f97c92adc077950c.png)

## Manage an Existing Subscription {#manage-an-existing-subscription}

You can easily update the payment information, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your Sonoran CAD subscription at anytime.

### 1. Access the Payment Center {#id-1.-access-the-payment-center-1}

Access the payment center on the Web or Desktop version of Sonoran CAD by going to [sonorancad.com/billing](https://sonorancad.com/billing), or by viewing the guide below.



<Card title="Accessing the Payment Center" href="/cad/pricing/faq/accessing-the-payment-center" />



### 2. Open the Stripe Customer Portal {#id-2.-open-the-stripe-customer-portal}

On any active subscription, select "Modify" to open the Stripe customer portal.

![Sonoran CAD Payment Center - Modify Subscription](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/72/72b38d679e3ab935f5f7fe6fdce003a752e821d45e980b12fa2670af3613fc79.png)

### 3. Utilize the Stripe Customer Portal {#id-3.-utilize-the-stripe-customer-portal}

Once opened, you can easily update your payment information, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel an existing subscription.

![Sonoran CAD - Stripe Customer Portal](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/87/875f9451138fff5539dfe7a3c46417775558dccc2f99cd592cb3f8fa795dac05.png)

## Migrate a Subscription to Another Community {#migrate-a-subscription-to-another-community}

Sonoran CAD subscriptions can be easily transferred from one community to another.

### 1. Access the Payment Center {#id-1.-access-the-payment-center-2}

Access the payment center on the Web or Desktop version of Sonoran CAD by going to [sonorancad.com/billing](https://sonorancad.com/billing), or by viewing the guide below.



<Card title="Accessing the Payment Center" href="/cad/pricing/faq/accessing-the-payment-center" />



### 2. Update the Subscription's Community ID {#id-2.-update-the-subscriptions-community-id}

Select "Set Community ID" on the subscription in your billing center.\
[Where can I find my community ID?](/cad/tutorials/getting-started/finding-your-community-id-and-authentication-code)

![Sonoran CAD Payment Center - Set Community ID](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/8f/8f8fd8589fe4c3aa87d72f87d34c4b5b0f04c58df9f9de136c75762eecb7e352.png)
