---

<Warning>

The payment center is only accessible from the **web or desktop version of Sonoran CAD**. The payment center **will not be visible using our iOS or Android** application.
</Warning>

### Access the Payment Center {#access-the-payment-center}

1. Sign into [sonorancad.com](https://app.sonorancad.com)
2. In the side navigation menu, select "My Account"

![Sonoran CAD - Access Account Menu](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/20/2051a8491af6a98a89a47a542e4cfddaee8bc6d04b40fe894336ac6f147ac9c5.png)

3\. Select "Subscriptions"\
\- This will open [sonorancad.com/billing](https://sonorancad.com/billing)<br />

![](https://docs-assets.sonoransoftware.com/sha256/4d/4d16691d14442261b0b5904b5d504ade5dd6830794932c6d21539b0125eafba9.png)
