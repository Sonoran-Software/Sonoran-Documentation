Last Updated: 8/21/2023

### **Sonoran Software Supports First Responders** {#sonoran-software-supports-first-responders}

As part of our commitment to serving the community, we deeply value the relentless effort and dedication displayed by our military and first responders, day in and day out. All U.S. military personnel, police officers, firefighters, and paramedics qualify for a 15% monthly discount.

To avail of this offer, create a ticket with our support team at [support.sonoransoftware.com](https://support.sonoransoftware.com/).

### Terms and Conditions {#terms-and-conditions}

Verify this offer with our support team by providing a valid ID and related documentation. We may grant or deny services and discounts at our discretion. The offer applies to Sonoran CAD, CMS, Radio, Store, and Five Bundle.
